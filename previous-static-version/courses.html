Level up, one artwork at a time

In a competitive world, strong creative ability is what differentiates the winners in all walks of life.

Stage 1 · Ages 5–73 levels

Foundation 🐣

Elementary concepts of drawing and colouring.

  • Level 1 & 2Basics of drawing & colouring — joining dots, cut & paste, completing drawings.
  • Level 3Higher complexity. Creativity is encouraged, with some guidelines still given.
🐣Foundation5–7 yrs
Stage 2 · After Foundation1 level

Pre-Basic 🔍

For children who have completed the Foundation levels.

  • Level 1Free-hand, observation and grid drawing using the elements of art — exploring objects, animals and human subjects.
🔍Pre-Basicbridge level
Stage 3 · Ages 8+3 levels

Basic 🖍️

Development of ideas, basic composition, drawing through shapes & references.

  • Level 1Basic skills of drawing, background creation and colouring.
  • Level 2Raises the bar — imagine different positions and depict an action sequence.
  • Level 3Human features from every angle — head, hair, face, hands, feet — characters across 3 generations and emotions.
🖍️Basic8+ yrs
Stage 4 · After Basic3 levels

Intermediate 🎭

More complex ideas, varied & detailed composition and mixed media in colouring.

  • Level 1Human-based themes through figures, characters & professions.
  • Level 2Human-figure illustrations based on different themes and concepts.
  • Level 3Explore and create complex day-to-day scenes from the child’s own life.
🎭Intermediatelevel up!
Stage 5 · After Intermediate3 levels

Advanced 🖌️

For students who complete Intermediate Level 3.

  • Drawing & SketchingDrawing through observation & references, with pencil shading techniques.
  • PaintingA composite knowledge of painting.
  • IllustrationDevelopment of ideas, advanced composition — posters & illustrations.
