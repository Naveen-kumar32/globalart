Why parents love Globalart

It’s more than just art. It’s a proven method that uses drawing & colour to build the skills your child needs everywhere — in school and in life.

💡

Out-of-the-box thinking

An enhanced level of creativity — kids learn to create, not copy. Every artwork is uniquely theirs.

🎯

Better focus

Improved observation and concentration that shows up in school work, projects and exams.

🦁

Confidence boost

Increased confidence and smartness — children proudly share ideas in class, plays and competitions.

🗣️

Self-expression

Higher levels of self-expression. Colouring helps kids “vent” feelings in a healthy, happy way.

🚀

Future careers

A foundation for advertising, animation, architecture, product, interior & fashion design and more.

😌

A happy stress-buster

inside a class

How the magic happens ✨

Small batches, trained child-friendly teachers and a structured syllabus with international teaching standards.

💭

Spark an idea

Each child comes up with their own idea for the theme — no copying from samples.

✏️

Draw it out

Teachers guide with simple techniques — finger drawing, point-to-point & drawing through shapes.

🎨

Colour & compose

Kids explore colours, backgrounds and composition with different media.

🏆

Proud creation

A one-of-a-kind artwork — and a little more confidence after every single class.

13 levels of fun

A creative journey from 5 to 15

Kids learn progressively — new themes, composition techniques and media at every level.

🐣Ages 5–7

Foundation

Dots, cut & paste, first colours. 3 levels.
🔍Bridge

Basic
