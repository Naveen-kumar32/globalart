our story

Born in 1999 from a love of art & children

Globalart is the first and only systematic, complete and efficient art programme that aims to develop the artistic and creative potential of children. Founder Mr. Mahair Goh has a passion for art and has worked with children for over two decades.

Extensive research produced a step-by-step syllabus that has become a trendsetter — systematic and straightforward, designed for every student, even those with little artistic talent. It gives children the confidence to excel in school and society.

“Art & Creativity can never be separated, one needs the other to function as a team to realise our imagination.”

— Mahair Goh, Founder
