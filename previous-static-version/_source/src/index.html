How the magic happens ✨

Small batches, trained child-friendly teachers and a structured syllabus with international teaching standards.

💭

Spark an idea

Each child comes up with their own idea for the theme — no copying from samples.

✏️

Draw it out

Teachers guide with simple techniques — finger drawing, point-to-point & drawing through shapes.

🎨

Colour & compose

Kids explore colours, backgrounds and composition with different media.

🏆

Proud creation

A one-of-a-kind artwork — and a little more confidence after every single class.
