🌟How is Globalart going to benefit my child?+
  • Enhanced level of creativity — “the ability to think out-of-the-box”
  • Higher levels of self-expression
  • Improved observation and better concentration
  • Increased confidence and smartness
  • Builds the foundation for varied career options in the future
  • Acts as an effective stress buster
🏫Will Globalart help in school?+

Definitely. Globalart fosters creativity, so over time children come up with creative ideas — in essay writing, simple experiments, group discussions & debates, and of course painting. Since the programme builds better observation and concentration, children fare well in different school activities.

🎂What is the recommended age to start, and why?+

The recommended age to start the Foundation course is 5 years. By then children have the fine motor skills to hold a pencil with a firm grip and can sit reasonably patiently for the 2-hour class. Foundation activities like joining dots and cutting / tearing paper further improve fine motor skills.

💭What is ‘Creativity’?+

Creativity is a mental and social process involving the discovery of new ideas or concepts, or new associations between existing ones. Simply put, it is the act of making something new.

🎯How is ‘Creativity’ different from ‘Talent’?+

Talent usually means being good in a certain field. Creativity is a point of view — an approach to the mundane that transforms an ordinary object into something truly unique. Creativity may be exhibited in every sphere of life.

🖍️Why more colouring than drawing in the Foundation levels?+
  • Children love colours and respond to them instantly. Colour helps them observe, categorise and organise the world around them.
  • Understanding colour is a tool for learning skills across the curriculum — from maths and science to language and reading.
  • Colouring helps children “vent” their feelings and emotions, and is a lovely way to de-stress after a busy morning of schoolwork.
✨How is Globalart different from other drawing courses?+
  • A well-defined and structured syllabus
  • Conforms to international teaching standards
  • Enhances mental development and broadens imagination & exploration
  • Enhances observation and concentration
  • Drawing, colouring and creativity are the evaluation parameters

Each child’s creation is unique — after every class they learn to create, not copy. We don’t teach art by copying a sample; we guide children to visualise, comprehend and experiment with subjects, backgrounds, colours and composition.

✂️Do you teach ‘short cuts’ on how to draw?+

We don’t teach shortcuts, because we believe all children are naturally creative — what’s simple for one child may not be for another, so they are guided proactively. We do teach easy, systematic ways to draw objects like the sun, trees, flowers, animals and people.

🎨What is the aim of the Globalart programme?+

To nurture the creativity and intellectual potential of our students using art as a tool — developing imagination and a more sensitive response to the natural and man-made world.

🗣️My child has great ideas but struggles to draw them. Can Globalart help?+

Yes! Once a child has their own idea for a theme, the instructor helps with simple techniques — finger drawing, point-to-point drawing or drawing through shapes — plus lots of questioning to compose the picture. Because it’s their own idea, children participate far better.
